---
title: "The Contract Is the Beginning"
description: "Why the signature is only the start of the relationship."
published: 2026-07-21
tags: [leadership, relationships, trust]
---
A signed contract can feel like the finish line. In my experience, it is usually the starting point.

The real test begins after the celebration—when problems appear, expectations shift and both sides learn whether the promises made during the sale survive delivery.

The best commercial relationships create one simple feeling for the customer: **this is one less thing I need to worry about.**
