The question connecting them

Is downstream AI demand growing fast enough to narrow the gap with the infrastructure being built and committed?

Why there is no gap percentage yet

Public data do not give us a clean, recurring view of AI-capable capacity that is live, committed, available and actually utilised. So the gap cannot be measured honestly as one number today. These two pages track the strongest observable evidence on either side instead.

What the explainer does

Builds the upstream mental model: conventional versus AI-optimised compute, the changing workload mix, power demand and the limits of vacancy data.

What the monitor does

Follows the downstream signals over time: whether businesses are adopting AI, deploying it more deeply, paying for it, using it more intensively and realising measurable value.
