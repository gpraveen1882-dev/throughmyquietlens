1 · Donor brief

IntentImprove access to early support before young people reach crisis.
PopulationYouth aged roughly 13–25, especially those facing barriers to help-seeking.
PreferenceSupport organisations with credible delivery capability and room to expand reach or strengthen evidence.
AvoidFunding a programme purely because it is visible, large, or emotionally compelling.

Research question

Which organisations appear sufficiently aligned and credible to merit deeper diligence — and what would we still need to know before recommending funding?

3 · Shortlist for deeper diligence
Priority 01

Harbour Youth Collective

Strong alignment with the donor’s early-intervention objective and credible evidence of reaching youth who may not enter formal care quickly.

Why progress

Clear target population, established delivery model, credible referral pathways.

What prevents a recommendation today

Need unit economics, capacity constraints, recent outcome data and a clearer view of what incremental funding changes.

Priority 02

BridgeLine

Potentially useful bridge between low-friction digital engagement and professional support, with a model that may expand reach efficiently.

Why progress

High accessibility, potential scalability, useful position earlier in the help-seeking journey.

What prevents a recommendation today

Need stronger evidence on conversion to appropriate care, safeguarding protocols and sustained outcomes.

Priority 03

Northstar Schools Network

Credible prevention model with institutional access through schools, but less certainty that the programme reaches the donor’s highest-priority underserved groups.

Why progress

Strong delivery infrastructure, repeatable model, measurable school-level participation.

What prevents a recommendation today

Need beneficiary mix, school selection logic and evidence of outcomes beyond participation.

4 · Questions the research should create

Questions for the organisations

  • What changes for beneficiaries that would not happen without your programme?
  • How do you know which youth you are not reaching?
  • What happens when demand exceeds current capacity?
  • What would an additional S$250k fund specifically?
  • Which outcome measure do you trust most, and where is the evidence weakest?

Questions for the donor or adviser

  • How much uncertainty are we comfortable funding?
  • Is the objective proven delivery, innovation, system change, or a portfolio across all three?
  • Would unrestricted funding be acceptable if the organisation is strong?
  • Do we care more about depth of outcome or breadth of reach?
  • What evidence would be sufficient to move from conversation to commitment?

Important: every organisation name and every organisation-level assessment on this page is synthetic and exists only to demonstrate the research workflow. This is not a rating of any real Singapore charity and should not be used to make a funding decision. A real assignment would preserve source links, distinguish verified facts from interpretation, document missing evidence, and require direct diligence before any recommendation.
