About the project →
1 · What has your client asked you to explore?

For this demo the cause and geography are fixed: youth mental wellbeing in Singapore. The point is to show how donor preferences change the research priority—not to claim one organisation is objectively “best”.

Research brief

Identify organisations helping young people access support early, with credible delivery capability and enough evidence to justify deeper diligence.

Research question

Which organisations merit more of the adviser’s time, and what still needs to be established before any funding recommendation?

5 · Questions the research should create

Questions for organisations

  • What changes for beneficiaries that would not happen without your programme?
  • How do you know which youth you are not reaching?
  • What would additional funding change specifically?
  • Which outcome measure do you trust most, and where is the evidence weakest?

Questions for the donor or adviser

  • How much uncertainty are we comfortable funding?
  • Do we care more about depth of outcome or breadth of reach?
  • Would unrestricted funding be acceptable if the organisation is strong?
  • What evidence would be sufficient to move from conversation to commitment?
