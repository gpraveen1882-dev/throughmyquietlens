## Merged rather than duplicated
Posts expressing the same central idea were retained in the strongest existing article or collection page. Repeated announcements, extracts and promotional versions were not turned into separate essays.

## Intentionally left on LinkedIn
- event reminders and temporary announcements
- congratulations and brief community acknowledgements
- repost commentary without a standalone evergreen argument
- job-search or role-change updates
- polls, engagement prompts and platform-specific calls for comments

These exclusions are editorial decisions, not missing migration work.

## Ongoing rule
The website is the canonical home for evergreen writing. LinkedIn remains a distribution and conversation channel. New evergreen posts should be added to the relevant collection; temporary posts can remain on LinkedIn only.
