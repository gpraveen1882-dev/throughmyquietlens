---
title: "Why I Dislike Networking"
description: "The relationships that mattered most to me were built through shared work, not exchanged cards."
published: 2026-07-10
tags: [relationships, community]
---
I have always been uncomfortable with the word *networking*. It can make relationships sound like assets collected for future use.

Most of the people who changed my life entered it differently: through working together, solving something difficult, or sharing a conversation without an agenda.
