Why I write

I did not begin writing because I had answers.

I began because slowing down long enough to put thoughts into words often revealed questions I had not realised I was asking.

Some essays come from boardrooms. Others from classrooms, conversations, family, or ordinary moments that quietly changed how I think. This website is where I continue that conversation.
