For more than forty years, I had never lived with a dog. Then Percy and Pluto arrived.

They did not change my life through a dramatic moment. They changed it through repetition: the greeting at the door, the insistence on a walk, the ability to forgive a delayed meal within seconds.

Some lessons arrive in classrooms. Others wait beside the door with a leash.
